The Brain

ESP32 Processing.

At its core, a highly efficient ESP32 microcontroller processes the mechanical flow data. Using a custom-built C++ firmware, it filters signal noise, calculates the precise volume, and packages the data.

Dual Core Processing

Handles flow counting interrupts and Wi-Fi transmission simultaneously.

API Ready

Pushes JSON payloads instantly to your local Python backend.

Adaptability

Every bathroom configuration is unique. Whether you attach the device to the high wall-mounted shower head or the lower handheld hose, the software adapts.

SaaS & Web App

The Digital Ecosystem.

Hardware is just the beginning. The Web App turns cold data into a highly engaging, gamified experience designed to build lasting ecological habits.

Local Python Server

Total privacy. Your data never leaves your home. The ESP32 communicates directly with a local Python Flask server, storing your entire shower history securely in a JSON database.

Eco-Coins Economy

Every liter saved below the 60L threshold generates Eco-Coins. Use this digital currency in Goutti's Shop to purchase exclusive backgrounds, crowns, and accessories for your avatar.

Quest Engine

The system generates Daily Quests, Squad Goals for the whole family, and Epic Career Trophies. Complete them to earn massive XP boosts and maintain your Fire Streak.

The Evolution of Goutti

Your actions have consequences. Save water, and watch your virtual seed grow through 5 stages into a majestic Forest Master. Waste water, and Goutti becomes stressed, locking access to premium features.
