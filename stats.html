Meet the Hardware

The core of the Water Mirror ecosystem. A sleek, waterproof IoT device powered by an ESP32 microcontroller, designed to integrate seamlessly into any bathroom.

ESP32 Powered Precision

Equipped with an advanced internal water flow sensor, the device measures your exact water consumption in real-time. The powerful ESP32 chip connects to your home Wi-Fi to silently push data directly to your local Python server.

Wi-Fi Sync C++ Firmware Real-Time Data

Smart Core

Ultra-low latency connection to the dashboard.

100% Adaptable Design

Every bathroom is different. That's why we engineered the Water Mirror device to be installed exactly where you need it: whether directly attached to the upper shower head, or connected to the lower handheld shower outlet.

Smart Screen Rotation

Installed it upside down for a better fit? No problem. Simply press and hold the Select button for 5 seconds. The digital display will instantly rotate 180°, allowing you to read your live stats comfortably in any configuration.

Flip Display

Hardware designed to fit your environment.

Gamification Engine

Once you step out of the shower, the hardware hands off the baton to our Web SaaS. Your liters are converted into XP, Eco-Coins, and Fire Streaks, unlocking cosmetic rewards and funding real-world NGO projects.
